# Caprae Capital — Business Understanding & Employment Details

## What is Caprae's mission?
Based on the assignment brief, Caprae Capital aims to create lasting value through strategic initiatives and operational improvement, not only through the transaction itself. It emphasizes helping companies use AI and practical technology to improve decision-making, streamline operations, and unlock growth opportunities. Its SaaS and M&A-as-a-Service approaches reflect the view that value creation continues well beyond acquisition.

## Why do you want to work at Caprae Capital?
I want to work at Caprae Capital because it connects software engineering with tangible business outcomes. I enjoy building full-stack products that reduce manual work, make information easier to act on, and help teams make better decisions. This assignment is especially interesting to me because it requires balancing product judgment, user experience, and engineering execution within a tight time limit. I would bring my development experience, curiosity, and willingness to take ownership while learning more about AI-enabled business transformation.

## How is Caprae changing the ETA space and broader PE?
Entrepreneurship through acquisition gives operators a path to acquire and run an existing business rather than start one from scratch. The assignment describes Caprae's approach as going beyond the transaction by supporting sourcing, operational initiatives, and the use of AI. Combining technology with hands-on business support can help acquisition entrepreneurs discover opportunities more efficiently and focus on improvements after closing. The distinctive emphasis in the brief is that M&A should be treated as a longer value-creation journey, rather than as a one-time transaction.
